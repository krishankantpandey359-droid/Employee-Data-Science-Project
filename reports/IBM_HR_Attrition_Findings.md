## Interpretation limits

- These are descriptive comparisons in a static public benchmark dataset. They are not an estimate of any current employer's attrition rate.
- Group differences do not establish why employees leave. Overtime, income, department, or satisfaction must not be treated as proven causes.
- Results are not suitable for individual employment decisions.
- The upstream documentation does not define every ordinal scale label or monetary unit. The analysis preserves original codes and avoids inventing definitions.
- No prediction model is trained or deployed in this report.
